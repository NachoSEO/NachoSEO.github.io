faq:
  - question: ccTLD, subdomain or subdirectory?
    answer: It depends. The subdirectory concentrates authority on one domain and is the easiest to maintain, but it shares the quality assessment with the whole site. At Softonic, the translations inside the main domain ended up lowering its overall quality, and moving them to their own ccTLDs made the main domain and each language grow. The answer depends mostly on the quality of the content in each language and the resources you have for each market.
  - question: Does hreflang improve rankings?
    answer: Not directly. It helps Google show each user the version in their language or country. Badly implemented, it makes the wrong version compete with the right one or makes none of them show up.
  - question: Can I translate the site with AI?
    answer: You can, but with review. Translating thousands of pages without supervision is exactly what Google gives as an example of low-effort content, and quality is measured at the site level, so weak translations can also drag down the original language.

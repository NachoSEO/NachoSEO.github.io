## The context

Softonic is one of the largest software download platforms in the world: a portfolio of six sites with over 1 billion users a year. This is SEO at massive scale: a template change propagates to hundreds of thousands of URLs in hours, and every percentage point of traffic is millions of visits. As SEO Manager, my job was to make that machine not just hold, but grow — in a market where Google changes the rules every few months.

## The problem

The catalog covered about 100,000 applications — a fraction of the software people search for every day. There was a huge opportunity to capture unmet demand, but producing useful content at that scale with manual processes was unworkable: too expensive, too slow, impossible to keep at quality.

## The system

I designed and led a large-scale content generation program — the most impactful initiative in the company during that period:

- **Demand detection**: semantic and programmatic keyword research to map what software people searched for and couldn't find in the catalog.
- **Our own models, before ChatGPT**: the program started when commercial LLMs didn't exist yet. We trained our own generation models, with their quality pipelines, when almost nobody was producing AI content at this scale.
- **Production at scale with quality control**: generation and review pipelines that kept the editorial standard without a human bottleneck.
- **SEO architecture**: templates, interlinking and structured data so every new page added authority to the whole.
- **Agile methodology**: sprints, per-batch metrics and decisions based on real performance, not intuition.

## The result

In two years the program went from 0 to **20 million monthly organic sessions**, and the catalog grew from 100,000 to **over 1 million pages**. In 2024, the portfolio's organic traffic grew **40% year over year**. The system kept producing after I left — that's the difference between a tactic and a system.

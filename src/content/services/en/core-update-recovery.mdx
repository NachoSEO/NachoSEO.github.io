faq:
  - question: How long does it take to recover traffic?
    answer: At Search Central Live Barcelona 2026, Google shared its internal timelines. After a core update, 3 to 6 months is typical, and in the worst case you have to wait for the next core update, 6 months to a year. Be wary of anyone who promises recovery in weeks.
  - question: Is a core update a penalty?
    answer: No. A manual action is a penalty, and it shows up in Search Console. A core update recalculates how Google values the quality of pages and of the site; if you drop, either other pages now look better to it than yours, or one part of your site has lowered the assessment of the rest.
  - question: What if I haven't changed anything on my site?
    answer: It can drop anyway. The index keeps growing, and a page can fall without getting worse because better ones appear. That is why the diagnosis compares your site with what ranks today for your queries.
  - question: Does deleting content help?
    answer: Sometimes. If weak page types are dragging down the good ones, consolidating or removing them can help. But deleting without a diagnosis can take good traffic with it. The decision is made page type by page type.
  - question: How much does it cost?
    answer: The diagnosis is a fixed project with a fixed price based on the size of the site. The follow-up monitoring, if you want it, is priced by month. I detail it in writing after the first call.

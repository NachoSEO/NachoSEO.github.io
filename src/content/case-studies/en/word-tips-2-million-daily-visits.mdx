## The context

word.tips is a tool for solving word games (Wordle, Scrabble and similar) — a market with enormous search demand and fierce competition. As Head of SEO at Cliqpod I led its organic strategy.

## The problem

At this volume, SEO isn't about tricks: it's about operating. A template mistake replicates across hundreds of thousands of URLs in hours. A bad architecture decision can cost millions of visits. And the word-games market rewards whoever answers each search pattern better and faster.

## The system

- **Programmatic content done right**: thousands of pages generated from data, each answering exactly the search that originates it — no thin content.
- **Architecture for scale**: URL structure and interlinking designed so crawl budget gets spent where it produces.
- **Continuous monitoring**: per-template performance tracking to catch problems before they scale.

## The result

The site reached **peaks of 2 million daily visits**, with virtually all traffic arriving through organic. The lesson I carry to every project: at this scale, systems and processes matter more than any individual trick.

## The problem

Your organic traffic is flat or falling, AI Overviews eat your clicks, and you can't tell whether your agency is doing 2019 SEO. Meanwhile, your competitors are starting to show up as the answer in ChatGPT and Perplexity.

## My approach

Systems SEO, not tricks: architecture, programmatic content where it makes sense, entity authority and structured data so both Google and generative engines understand you and cite you. It's the same methodology I used to grow Softonic's catalog from 100,000 to over 1 million useful pages and its content program from 0 to 20 million monthly sessions.

## The context

word.tips is a tool for solving word games (Wordle, Scrabble and similar), a market with huge search demand and a lot of competition. Between 2024 and 2025, as Head of SEO at Cliqpod, I led its organic strategy.

## The problem

At this volume, SEO is mostly day-to-day operations. A template mistake replicates across hundreds of thousands of URLs in hours. A bad architecture decision can cost millions of visits. And the word-games market rewards whoever answers each search pattern better and faster.

## The system

- **Programmatic content done right**: thousands of pages generated from data, each built to answer one specific search, with no empty pages.
- **Architecture for scale**: URL structure and internal links designed so Google crawls the pages that bring traffic first.
- **Continuous monitoring**: per-template performance tracking to catch problems before they grow.

## The result

**I doubled organic traffic** and the site reached **peaks of 2 million daily visits**, with almost all traffic coming from organic search. What I take to every project is that, at this scale, processes count for more than any single trick.

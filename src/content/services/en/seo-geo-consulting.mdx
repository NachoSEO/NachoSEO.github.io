    answer: SEO ranks you in Google's results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) aims to get answer engines to cite you directly. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) works so that models like ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews use you as a source and recommend you. I work on all three at once because your customers already search in all three places.

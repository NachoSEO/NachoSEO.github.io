## The problem

Your team loses hours every week on tasks a machine would do better: reports, repetitive content, lead triage, moving data between tools. Meanwhile you pay for SaaS subscriptions you barely use.

## My approach

I don't sell "a chatbot". I map your operation, find the processes with the highest cost per hour and build the smallest system that removes them: AI agents with clear limits, automations connected to your real data, and custom software where generic SaaS falls short.

It's the same work I do as Head of AI at Reverse Tech: systems that execute real marketing and operations work, not demos.

## What you get

- **Process audit**: what to automate first and how much each piece saves.
- **Agentic systems in production**: research, content, CRM, reporting or support executed by agents, with human oversight where it matters.
- **SaaS replacement**: software that does exactly what you need, without plan limits or per-seat pricing.
- **Your code, your docs**: no black boxes, no vendor lock-in.

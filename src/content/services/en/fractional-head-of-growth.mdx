## The problem

You spend on marketing but nobody senior answers for the overall result. Every channel runs on its own, agencies grade their own homework, and you can't tell what's actually working.

## My approach

I step in as your growth director: audit the channels, cut what doesn't perform, set the strategy and lead execution with your team or whoever we need to hire. I've managed entire growth orgs — SEO, affiliates, email, social, YouTube — and I know what a healthy channel looks like from the inside.

## What you get

- **A growth strategy** prioritized by revenue impact, not trends.
- **Team leadership**: hiring, mentoring and processes so the team performs without you chasing it.
- **A measurement system**: channel dashboard with clear goals and monthly reviews.
- **Execution**: not just advice; I work with the team until the machine runs.

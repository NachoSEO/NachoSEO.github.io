faq:
  - question: When should I bring you in?
    answer: As early as possible, ideally when the migration is decided and before the new site is designed. Many problems (URLs that disappear, templates without content, JavaScript Google cannot see) are cheap to avoid in design and expensive to fix after launch.
  - question: Is it normal to lose traffic after a migration?
    answer: There are usually a few weeks of movement while Google crawls and reprocesses the new URLs. What is not normal is a drop that does not recover, and it almost always comes from badly built redirects, content that gets lost or pages that stop being linked.
  - question: What types of migration do you handle?
    answer: Changes of domain, CMS or platform, URL structure, HTTP to HTTPS, merging several sites into one or the reverse, and changes to the international structure (subdomains, subdirectories or ccTLDs).
  - question: Who implements the redirects?
    answer: Your development team or your vendor. I give them the map, check that it is implemented correctly before and after launch, and answer their questions.
  - question: How much does it cost?
    answer: It depends on the number of URLs, how many templates change and whether the migration is only technical or also involves content. I send you a fixed proposal after the first call.

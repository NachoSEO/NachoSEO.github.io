  - question: What is an agentic system and what is it for in my company?
    answer: It is software that uses AI models to run complete processes within the limits you define, such as researching, writing, classifying, publishing or replying. It lets processes that eat your team's hours today run on their own and scale without hiring.
  - question: Can a custom automation replace my SaaS tools?
    answer: Often, yes. If you pay for several subscriptions and use a fifth of each, your own system that does exactly what you need usually costs less per year and has no plan, seat or credit limits. I review your tools and tell you what is worth replacing and what is not.
  - question: What do I need to get started?
    answer: A process that hurts because it is repetitive, slow or expensive. On the first call we map it, I estimate the savings and propose the smallest system that solves it. You don't need your own technical team.
  - question: Who maintains the system once it's built?
    answer: The code and data are yours, with no dependency on me or third parties. I can handle improvements or leave it documented for your team, whichever you prefer.
  - question: How much does it cost?
    answer: It depends on the process and how much needs building. After the first call I send you a written proposal with scope, timelines and price.

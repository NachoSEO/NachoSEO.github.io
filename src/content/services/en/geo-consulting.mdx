faq:
  - question: Do I need an llms.txt?
    answer: Today there is no evidence it helps. In its guide to AI features, Google says no special file is needed to appear, and there is no record that it uses one. If you want one, it costs little, but it is not what will get you to show up.
  - question: How do you measure AI visibility?
    answer: By running the same questions many times and counting in how many answers you appear, because each answer is a different sample. I measure by topic, with fixed questions, in ChatGPT, AI Mode and AI Overviews, and I separate when it names you, when it links you and what it says about you.
  - question: How long does it take to see anything?
    answer: It depends where. What the AI pulls from searching the web (AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT with search) can move in weeks. What the model knows from memory only changes when it is retrained, and that takes months.
  - question: Do you guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend me?
    answer: No. Nobody controls what a model answers. What I do is measure where you stand, find which sources decide your sector, work to get you into them, and measure whether it works.
  - question: How much does it cost?
    answer: It depends on how many topics and markets we measure and how much site and mentions work we do together. I send you a fixed proposal after the first call.

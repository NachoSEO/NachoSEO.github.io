faq:
  - question: What's the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?
    answer: SEO ranks your site in Google's results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) gets answer engines to cite you directly. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) optimizes so generative models like ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews use you as a source and recommend you. I work all three layers at once, because your customer now asks in all three places.
  - question: Is SEO still worth it in the AI era?
    answer: Yes, but the split has changed. Simple informational queries get absorbed by AI; commercial research and transactional searches still go through classic results, and generative engines cite whoever has real authority. The right strategy today covers both fronts — ignoring either one leaves money on the table.
  - question: What size of website do you work with?
    answer: From niche sites to platforms with millions of pages. I've led SEO for softonic.com (a portfolio with 1B+ annual users) and word.tips, with peaks of 2 million daily visits. The systems scale in both directions.

## The context

As Head of AI (Marketing Automation) at Reverse Tech, the paid channel needed a volume of creatives no human team could produce at a reasonable cost. The answer wasn't hiring more people: it was building a factory.

## The problem

Creative production was the bottleneck of the entire channel. Every concept went through briefing, design, review and manual upload to the platforms. The learning about what worked stayed in each trafficker's head, and the external tools promising to solve it cost tens of thousands of euros a year without giving us control over the engine.

## The system

A closed loop, from signal to published ad:

- **Winner mining**: the system analyzes the creatives performing in our own account and in competitors', and turns them into actionable patterns.
- **From pattern to brief**: those patterns become concrete production briefs.
- **Smart routing**: each asset is assigned to a human or to the generative engine depending on its complexity. The routing rules live in our platform, not a vendor's.
- **Shipping via API**: finished assets enter the Meta and Google ad ecosystems directly through their APIs.
- **Learning loop**: each ad's performance flows back into the system and feeds the next round of concepts.

## The result

Production went from **~200 creatives per week to over 3,000**. Output per person rose roughly tenfold, and not just on trafficking and uploading: on creation, which was the real test. Building it in-house also removed **~€20k/month** in external platform costs, with the engine and the data fully owned.
